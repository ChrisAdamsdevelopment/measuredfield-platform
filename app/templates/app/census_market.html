How to read these figures

A clearer picture, not a prediction.

Employer establishments are workplaces with employees, not necessarily distinct companies. Nonemployer establishments have no paid employees.

These datasets cover different years and industry-code versions. We keep the figures separate rather than claim they represent a current total of competitors.

Payroll and gross receipts in Census source data are recorded in thousands of dollars. The table keeps those original units visible.
