What a connection allows

For now, the MeasuredField ChatGPT connector is designed for read-only business and inquiry information. It does not send customer replies, approve changes, publish websites, access your entire computer or authorize paid AI searches.

Live ChatGPT sign-in and plugin installation are still being prepared. This page lets you disconnect any verified account links after that service becomes available. Computer permissions are managed separately under Computers.
