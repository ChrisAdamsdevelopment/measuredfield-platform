Straight answers

Questions owners ask

Do I need an existing website or business?

No. You can start with a name and a list of what you offer. If you already have a business and a site, we start from what's there.

Will you promise more customers?

No. We can make it easier for people to reach you and harder for inquiries to get lost, and we show you the real counts. We don't forecast revenue or rankings.

Is this an AI product?

You're buying a better-run business, not a technology. Software does a lot of the work behind the scenes, and nothing is published or sent to your customers without your approval.

Who can see my business information?

You and the team members you invite. Each business has its own private workspace, kept separate from every other customer's inquiries, documents and settings. See our privacy information.

Can I leave?

Yes. You can export everything in your workspace and request deletion from your settings at any time.
