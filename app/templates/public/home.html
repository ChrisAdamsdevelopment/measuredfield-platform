Practical examples

What an improvement looks like.

These are illustrations of the kind of change we make. They are not customer results, and we don't quote results we haven't measured.

Illustration · home services

"I lose track of who I'm supposed to call back."

Before
Inquiries arrive by text, voicemail and a contact form that sends email. Follow-up lives in the owner's head.
Change
The website form feeds an inquiry desk. Each request gets a status and a follow-up date; the owner sees what's due today.
What becomes measurable
How many inquiries arrive, and how many have a recorded first response.
Illustration · new business

"I'm just starting and have nothing online."

Before
A business name, a list of services and a phone number.
Change
A professional site drafted from those details, previewed and approved by the owner, published at its own address.
What becomes measurable
Visits to the site, how many people start the contact form, and how many finish it.
Illustration · trades

"Quotes take me all evening."

Before
Each estimate is typed from scratch and approved by a reply text that's hard to find later.
Change
Itemized proposals built in the workspace. The customer approves from a link and the approval is recorded with a name and date.
What becomes measurable
Proposals sent, approved and declined.
Illustration · small team

"Job details are scattered across five apps."

Before
Customer information in one place, documents in another, notes on paper.
Change
Customers, projects and documents in one private workspace, with staff and read-only access for the team.
What becomes measurable
What's recorded and who is handling it. We don't claim time savings we can't observe.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask

Do I need an existing website or business?

No. You can start with a name and a list of what you offer. If you already have a business and a site, we start from what's there.

Will you promise more customers?

No. We can make it easier for people to reach you and harder for inquiries to get lost, and we show you the real counts. We don't forecast revenue or rankings.

Is this an AI product?

You're buying a better-run business, not a technology. Software does a lot of the work behind the scenes, and nothing is published or sent to your customers without your approval.

Who can see my business information?

You and the team members you invite. Each business has its own private workspace, kept separate from every other customer's inquiries, documents and settings. See our privacy information.

Can I leave?

Yes. You can export everything in your workspace and request deletion from your settings at any time.
