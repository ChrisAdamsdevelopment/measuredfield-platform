04 / MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Local business landscapes

Explore selected U.S. markets using Census Bureau public aggregates. Employer-business evidence from 2023 County Business Patterns is distinct from nonemployer-business evidence from 2024 Nonemployer Statistics. The dashboard preserves sources, reference years and geographic limits.

07 / CHATGPT

Ask questions in natural language

The private MeasuredField ChatGPT plugin is being connected to the authenticated MCP service. Its initial read-only tools are designed to recognize the actual signed-in owner and their permitted business. Availability depends on OAuth verification and plugin access—not simply having a ChatGPT account.

Private connection pilot · not generally available
08 / YOUR COMPUTER

Scoped desktop collaboration

Optional computer pairing is designed around explicit device confirmation, narrow folder permissions and revocation. Installing the website alone does not grant MeasuredField access to local files or browsers.

Permissioned development pilot
09 / EMAIL & GOOGLE DATA

Connect authorized business data

Gmail and Google Search Console can add useful context when each business authorizes them through an appropriate connection. These provider integrations are being verified separately and are not automatically enabled in the current beta.

Provider-dependent integrations · not active by default
