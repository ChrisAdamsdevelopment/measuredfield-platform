FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you commit.

Do I have to replace my existing website?

No. Start by identifying what already works. A focused improvement may be more valuable than a complete rebuild.

Can you promise the top three Google Maps positions?

No. Google controls ranking and considers relevance, distance, and prominence. We can improve accurate business information and assess observed performance, but no one can honestly guarantee local-pack placement.

Is the free visibility example a real Google or AI audit?

No. The public visibility preview is not a live scan. It is an illustrative example built from the information you enter. Actual search/AI visibility research requires approved providers, evidence sources, and a separately verified process.

What does the initial assessment cost?

The two-question assessment is free. Optional managed implementation and subscriptions are separate; scope and pricing should be confirmed before paid work begins.
