## Google Business Profile: eligibility gate
Official Google rule: ordinary profiles require genuine in-person customer contact during stated hours, and online-only SaaS operations do not qualify (https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036). If MeasuredField provides real in-person consultations/service visits, it may qualify as a service-area business with residential verification address hidden from public (https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481). Verify the actual brand, allowed primary category chosen to reflect real work, customer contact phone, genuine service hours/area and identity evidence. A fake Prescott pin, virtual office, mailbox, public home address, keyword-stuffed company title or duplicate brand profile is not acceptable. Owner-controlled verification may require a live unedited mobile video >=30 seconds, not an AI ad (https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705). No GBP created in this story pending actual facts.
