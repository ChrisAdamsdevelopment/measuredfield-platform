# MF-049 — Local Search Map & evidence-to-action product integration

**Status:** Source implementation in private development only. Not a publicly released service, verified ranking provider integration, customer OAuth connection, or production deployment.

## Owner experience

The new authenticated **Local Search Map** under `/app/modules/local-search` accepts a CSV from an owner-approved SERP rank vendor. Up to 500 source rows or 300 distinct coordinate/query samples are validated and grouped by up to eight cities. Separate surfaces `organic` and `local_pack` avoid conflating map pack and organic results. Competing business positions may be supplied at the **same** city, coordinate, phrase, surface, provider and timestamp. Multiple competitor rows at one point do not multiply the owner's own sample count. Empty positions remain unknown—not silently treated as failures or rank 101. Imported positions do not become authenticated evidence merely because they have a vendor name.

The screen renders per-city mean imported position, top-3 and top-10 shares, source coordinate sample cells, pairwise competitor counts and a deterministic **Next-Step Intelligence** plan. Every suggested action includes the observed reason, a recommended next inspection and an evidence/uncertainty label. The tool never edits a listing, sends an email, publishes a site or spends a research budget automatically. Data remains in the current browser/form response; no new DB writes or cross-tenant sharing. This remains a development-only route, authenticated through the platform's existing membership and CSRF controls. All untrusted labels are rendered via `textContent`, not inserted as HTML.

CSV columns:

`city,keyword,surface,latitude,longitude,position,competitor,competitor_position,checked_at,provider`

- `surface` must be `organic` or `local_pack`; `position` and `competitor_position` are blank/unknown or integers 1..100.
- Coordinates must be finite and within WGS84 latitude/longitude ranges. Times must be timezone-aware ISO 8601 and provider labels nonblank. Filename/extensions are not authority or evidence.
- A valid report may be **factual third-party observations as imported**, but MeasuredField has not verified its source cryptographically or through an authenticated provider connection. The UI labels the entire report *owner-imported/unverified*.
- These observations are NOT Google Search Console average position, search results generated by a generic chatbot, Places API relevance order, or independently authenticated organic/Local Pack positions.

## The geographic ranking question

The earlier MF-021 search matrix **already supports** coordinate anchors, phrase variations, provider capability checks, locale and device rules, deduplicated signed request plans, and evidence-source labeling. It has **never yet executed a real paid Google SERP or Maps grid**. Generic Python HTTP requests to `google.com/search` and artificial coordinates do not guarantee Google interprets the requester as being physically located there. Automated scraping of Google ranking results without permission violates Google Search spam policies:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

We should connect the MF-021 matrix to a licensed geographic **SERP** provider that explicitly accepts latitude/longitude or documented location codes (e.g. DataForSEO's Maps, Local Finder, Local Pack, and Organic APIs). Every provider result needs a raw authenticated receipt, actual parameters used, search surface, timestamp, paid request counter, provider cost record, and repeat sampling. Do not use a Google Places Text Search relevance score as if it were a map-pack rank. Official Places has location bias and distance/relevance preference, but is a *different search product*:
https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/text-search
https://dataforseo.com/help-center/track-local-pack-local-finder-and-google-maps-rankings-with-api

Google Search Console Search Analytics can group by query/page/country/device/time, **not searcher city coordinates**. It is valuable as owned-site performance data, but does not answer a Prescott-vs-Prescott Valley grid question by itself:
https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/searchanalytics/query

## Design & positioning

The public homepage and owner module collection now describe one coherent system:

1. **Local Search Map:** rank evidence by city and sampled coordinates, owner-import preview. Live provider collection not enabled.
2. **AI Visibility Lab:** source-linked Search Console CSV analysis, controlled multi-market question plans, evidence provenance; no live provider result attestation.
3. **Lead Rescue / Customer Operations:** existing inquiry status, follow-up and CRM workflow, with a local opportunity-intelligence preview.
4. **Website Studio:** controlled preview, version/approval and proposal operations within the existing workspace.
5. **Computer Connect:** Windows owner-approved folder and edit-proposal private pilot; a full public ChatGPT-to-computer remote connection is NOT presently launched.
6. **Next-Step Intelligence:** explainable evidence-to-priority reasoning in the new geographic comparison tool, not a fully autonomous or independently verified strategic adviser.

The central differentiator is not that MeasuredField speaks with ChatGPT; it provides governed, business-specific context across *owned customer records, approved linked data and observed outcomes* so guidance is more useful than unsupported conversational guesses. Separate observation/interpretation/decision/action, show underlying source and date, and require owner approval for changes. Claims about rankings, leads or saved time require verified outcomes.

## Competing products worth learning from

- **Local Falcon:** geo-grid rank visualization, scheduled monitoring, local search/AI integrations. Its depth underscores why a basic grid alone is not defensible.
- **BrightLocal:** broad review/listing tools, local keyword and grid rank tracking, recent AI visibility analysis.
- **Whitespark and Local Dominator:** targeted grid/rank tracking at low introductory price points.
- **DataForSEO:** coordinate-scoped SERP API backend suitable for integrating provider observations with our proprietary owner-facing workflows.

Source guide: https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/geo-grid-ranking-tool-comparison-guide/

Avoid competing on sheer grid-point count alone. The strongest product bet is **search evidence + lead outcome + recommended action + approval + follow-up measurement**: owner immediately sees what should change, why, whether it was completed and whether the change improved inquiries.

## Prioritized follow-on modules

1. **Verified Geo Grid Adapter:** licensed SERP source, budget authorization, matched date/device/coordinates and citations, repeatable multi-city heat map. Nonnegotiable for real ranking claims.
2. **Opportunity-to-Action Engine:** consolidate true first-party leads, Search Console, rank samples, website publishing events, and human-reviewed improvements into a prioritized work queue with expected cost/effort and measured outcomes.
3. **Review & Reputation Response Center:** consented Google Business Profile/review connection; review response drafts and owner approval, not automated review solicitation to fake accounts.
4. **Conversion & Attribution Inspector:** phone tap/form funnel/inquiry source, privacy-conscious, no artificial revenue assumptions.
5. **Local Landing Page Gap Planner:** verified service/area landing page evidence, duplicate-content safeguards and owner-approved content tasks.
6. **CRM/Calendar/Email Connectors:** single consented owner setup, least-privilege per-connector scopes, action previews and immediate disconnect; avoid unlimited agent authority.
7. **Competitive Change Watch:** dated evidence snapshots of rivals' visible offerings, not invented business intelligence, with licensing and retention controls.

## ChatGPT connection is a separate release gate

OpenAI's current custom MCP ChatGPT connection documentation describes direct custom server creation for Business/Enterprise/Edu workspace admins and selected supported read/fetch cases for Pro; full remote write/modification MCP capabilities are not general-purpose Plus custom-server settings. See:
https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-connecting-custom-mcp-servers-in-chatgpt
https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256-plugins-in-chatgpt

For broad customer reach, investigate a supported *published* Plugin/ChatGPT Site or first-party hosted assistant route rather than requiring every owner to administer a Business ChatGPT workspace. Packaging cannot bypass account authorization, signed OAuth audience/scopes, owner consent and platform policy. The current repository's standalone MCP service has passing offline HTTP auth/protocol tests but no configured real OAuth provider, verified HTTPS staging deployment, registered ChatGPT connection or completed real desktop Secure MCP Tunnel test. Do not describe these as finished.
